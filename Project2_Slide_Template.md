# Project 2 Slide Template

Use short bullets, large font, and one clear chart per slide when possible.

## Slide 1 - Title
- Project title
- Your names
- Course and date

## Slide 2 - Dataset Background
- Dataset name
- Kaggle link
- What the dataset contains
- Why the topic matters

## Slide 3 - Project Goal
- Main research question
- 2-3 supporting questions

## Slide 4 - Data Overview
- Number of rows and columns
- Main variable groups
- Target variable
- Data types

## Slide 5 - Data Cleaning And Preprocessing
- Missing values found
- Duplicate rows removed
- Categorical encoding
- Outlier handling method

## Slide 6 - EDA Finding 1
- Insert first key chart
- 2-3 bullets explaining the finding

## Slide 7 - EDA Finding 2
- Insert second key chart
- 2-3 bullets explaining the finding

## Slide 8 - EDA Finding 3
- Insert correlation heatmap or grouped comparison chart
- 2-3 bullets explaining the finding

## Slide 9 - Main Insights
- Top patterns discovered
- Which factors looked most related to injury
- Why those findings matter

## Slide 10 - Value, Ethics, And Next Steps
- Value to teams, trainers, or society
- Ethical considerations
- Limitations
- Next steps

## Presentation Tips
- Do not use paragraphs on slides.
- Keep chart labels readable.
- Highlight only the strongest findings.
- Practice a 5-7 minute version and a shorter backup version.
